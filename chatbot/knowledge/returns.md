# Returns and cancellations

Orders can be cancelled until the shipment has been dispatched. Once the item has been shipped, cancellation is not available through the account dashboard.

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for eligible items that are unopened, unused, and in original condition. Damaged or incorrect items are reviewed separately and may be replaced or refunded after inspection.

The customer can request a return from the order timeline, and the support team will guide the next step.
