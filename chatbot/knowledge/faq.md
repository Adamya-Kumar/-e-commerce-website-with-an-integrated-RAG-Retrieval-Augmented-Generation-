# Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?
Customers can view the current status in their account order history and timeline after the order is placed.

Can I cancel my order after it is shipped?
No. Cancellation is allowed only until the item has been shipped.

What is the return window?
Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for eligible products in original condition.

Does Spark Commerce offer COD?
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for eligible products within India.
