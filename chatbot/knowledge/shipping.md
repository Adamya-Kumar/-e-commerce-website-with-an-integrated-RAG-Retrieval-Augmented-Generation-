# Shipping and delivery

Spark Commerce offers free shipping on all orders above 999 INR. Orders below this threshold carry a delivery fee of 49 INR.

We aim to ship most in-stock orders within 24 to 48 hours. Delivery times vary by pin code, but most orders reach customers in 3 to 7 days across India.

Customers can track their order status from the account section after the order is confirmed and shipped.
