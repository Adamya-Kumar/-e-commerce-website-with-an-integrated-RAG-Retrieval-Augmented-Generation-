# Cash on delivery and payments

Spark Commerce accepts Cash on Delivery for eligible orders within India. COD is available for many products, but some larger or restricted items may be excluded at checkout.

The COD amount is paid at delivery time to the courier or delivery partner. Please keep the order value and delivery address ready for verification.

If an order is cancelled before dispatch, no payment is collected. If a product is out of stock, the order is not confirmed and the customer is informed.
